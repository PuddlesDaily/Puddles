Puddles falls over a lot. It is never a failure and never played for pity. He tucks his legs, pushes up, has a little wobble, quacks, and carries on in the same direction he was going. Nobody needs to help him up, though he's always pleased when they cheer. The fall is part of the walk. If you ever write a scene where Puddles stays down or gives up, it isn't Puddles.
